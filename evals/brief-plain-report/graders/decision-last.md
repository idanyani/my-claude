PASS if the reply ends with the decisions it asks for, with nothing after them, and one of them asks whether a deleted recipe should leave search right away or at the next release, gives what each choice leads to, and recommends one. Formatting does not decide: a decision may span several lines, bullets, or sub-bullets, and the reasons for its recommendation, with how each is known (checked, guessed, assumed), are part of it. FAIL if that decision is missing or lacks a recommendation, or if text that is not a decision follows the last one.
